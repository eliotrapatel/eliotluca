THE ENTITY is a research platform for investors in AI. It models the whole AI supply chain as one graph: 835 companies, 645 of them listed, placed on 80 chain nodes and joined by 2,808 links. Every night the graph is re-scored as filings, prices and news land. What comes out answers three questions: which part of the chain is running short, where the shortage moves next, and which companies gain pricing power or get squeezed. The figures on this page are as of October 2026.

## Why the supply chain

Most AI analysis starts with models and tickers. I started with the things that stop a model from being trained or served. The real limits are physical: the power a data centre can draw, the grid connections that gate it, the memory and packaging beneath the chips, the networking that wires thousands of chips into one machine. When one of those runs short, the pressure travels along the chain and lands on companies that rarely appear in the same sentence. A graph can show that path. A list of stocks cannot.

## How it works

The method has three steps.

**Map the chain.** Everything AI runs on is drawn as 80 nodes across five layers: energy, chips, infrastructure, models and applications. Every company sits on the nodes it supplies or depends on, and 624 real facilities are placed on a globe, so a constraint can be traced to a place as well as to a name.

**Score the pressure.** Live news, market data and filings are read against the graph to find which node is running tight and how much weight sits on it. The whole graph is re-scored every night.

**Run it forward.** The model runs the chain forward to see where the next squeeze lands, a quarter or two ahead, and which of the 835 companies it touches.

## What the investor gets

A straight read on each name: the Verdict scores every listed AI company from 0 to 100 and attaches a call. A view of what elite funds and insiders are buying, dated from their public filings. A forecast of where the next shortage lands. And a Copilot that answers a question in plain language by composing a live dashboard from the graph, with its sources shown.

It is built to an institutional standard and written in plain English: if you follow AI in the news, you can use it.

## Accountability

A model that makes calls owes a record of them. Every call on THE ENTITY is recorded the day it is made, frozen, and graded in public, misses included. Every figure is labelled as fact, estimate or model, dated, and graded for confidence, and the methodology is published. It is funded only by subscriptions: no company can pay to look better in the model.

This is a habit from deal work. An information memorandum is only as good as the questions it survives. A map of the AI supply chain is only as good as the calls it lets other people check.

## The boundary

THE ENTITY is research, not investment advice. It shows where companies sit on the chain, with its sources; what a reader does with it is theirs. Every plan is paid, from $19 a month.
