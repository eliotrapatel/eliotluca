THE ENTITY is the institutional-grade map of the AI supply chain, built for investors. It holds 835 companies, 645 of them listed, placed on 80 chain nodes across five layers: energy, chips, infrastructure, models and applications. Between those nodes run 2,808 calibrated links. The current model is 2.8.

## Why the supply chain

Most AI analysis starts with models and tickers. I started with the things that stop a model from being trained or served. The real limits are physical: the power a data centre can draw, the grid connections that gate it, the memory and packaging beneath the chips, the networking that wires thousands of chips into one machine. When one of those runs short, the pressure travels along the chain and lands on companies that rarely appear in the same sentence. A graph can show that path. A list of stocks cannot.

## How it works

The method has three steps.

**Map the chain.** Every company sits on the nodes it supplies or depends on, and every link between nodes is calibrated. 624 facilities are geolocated across 74 geographies, so a constraint can be traced to a place as well as to a name. 48 chokepoints are identified on the map.

**Score the pressure.** The whole graph is re-scored every night. 103 elite funds are tracked and 92 of them are scored, and 871 narratives carry their sources.

**Run it forward.** The model looks for where pressure is likely to land next.

On top of the graph sits a Copilot. The same data is open through a public API, for anyone who would rather build on it than read it through the interface.

## Accountability

A model that makes calls owes a record of them. Every call on THE ENTITY is recorded the day it is made and graded in public, misses included. Every figure is labelled as fact, estimate or model, dated and graded for confidence, so a reader always knows what kind of number they are looking at and how much weight it can bear. The methodology and a backtest are published for the same reason: if I am wrong, it should be easy to see where.

This is a habit from deal work. An information memorandum is only as good as the questions it survives. A map of the AI supply chain is only as good as the calls it lets other people check.

## The boundary

THE ENTITY is educational analysis, not investment advice. It does not know anyone's situation. Plans start at $19 a month.
