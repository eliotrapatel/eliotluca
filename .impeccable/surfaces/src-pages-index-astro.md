# Surface brief: eliotluca.com (home and every route of the site)

Scope: the whole personal site. Home is Experience mode (the work leads), essays are Read, instruments are Operate, /links is a fast link-in-bio list.
Audience and job: investors and operators checking who Eliot is in under a minute on a phone; ORBES and THE ENTITY visitors looking for the founder; readers of his essays.
Action: open THE ENTITY or ORBES, read an essay, write to Eliot.
Proof on hand: real facts from PRODUCT.md only (ENTITY counts, ORBES draw, career, disciplines with entry years). No photos, no testimonials.
Build path: code-led (no image generation on this machine). Owner asked for full autonomy, so the direction was decided without a decision page; recorded here as the assumption.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A credible, quiet personal site for a finance-trained founder, set on prepared blue paper in iron-gall ink. The range is proven by facts (roles, ventures, a dated disciplines chart, working instruments), and Leonardo appears only as a restrained signature: one skeletal polyhedron and, on About, a few precise studies. Owner feedback 2026-10-08: "more credible, less mad scientist". It refuses both the headshot-bio-card-grid template and the eccentric notebook.

OWN-WORLD: Drenched carta da zucchero ground (dusty prepared blue), brown-black iron-gall ink for type and line, lead-white heightening for highlights, hatching and active states, metalpoint blue-grey for secondary lines. Night: indigo carta with heightening as line. Square corners, 1px ink rules, paper fibre and faint chain lines. Castoro Titling inscriptional capitals for the name and wordmark only; EB Garamond sentence case for page and section titles, EB Garamond for reading and italic annotations, Mona Sans for interface, numbers and dimension labels. Controls are engraved scales and ruled fields; links underline in ink and take a heightening wash on hover.

STORY: In one glance the visitor sees range drawn with the same precision, reads that Eliot works where capital, software and objects meet, then meets the two plates (THE ENTITY, ORBES) with live facts, the ledger of his finance record, the numbered run of essays and the working instruments, and ends at a plain way to write to him.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Desktop: thin top band, wordmark left, nav right. Name in inscriptional capitals on one line top-left (cols 1-7), one EB Garamond claim, a two-line intro, a ruled four-row roles table (THE ENTITY, ORBES, Amundi Technology, Novastone Capital Advisors) and text actions. The polyhedron sits right (cols 9-12) as the single drawing, quiet caption. Mobile: name across the width on one line, claim, intro, roles; no drawing.

FORM: Candidate 7 of 7 on the ordered list (Leonardo's notebook sheet), seed ad462f49. Raises: from the forge, every drawing carries real dimension lines; from the gravity garden, the polyhedron study turns under the visitor's hand; from the ice press, essays run as a numbered issue sequence; from BLAST, manifesto titles get full typographic commitment on their own pages; from the algorave floor, one clock drives every live element (the escapement ticks on the real second); from the step row, one unbroken timeline rule from 2016 to now carries a "now" mark.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

## Signature interaction and motion grammar

Signature: the polyhedron study (Leonardo's skeletal rhombicuboctahedron for Pacioli's De divina proportione) rotates under pointer drag or arrow keys; the escapement wheel advances one tooth per real second. One orchestrated load: ink lines draw across the sheet in reading order (~1.6 s), heightening settles last. Everything is visible without JS and static under reduced motion.
